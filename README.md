# Supply Chain & Inventory Analysis 

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **supply chain and inventory performance** using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**. The analysis was performed on a dataset containing **15,000 records** to identify patterns in inventory, sales, product profitability, supplier performance, and shipping time.

The final outcome is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to present key business metrics and support data-driven inventory and supply chain decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze inventory levels and stock availability
* Identify overstock and understock conditions
* Analyze product sales and profitability
* Evaluate supplier shipping performance
* Compare stock available with units sold
* Analyze revenue across product categories
* Create an interactive dashboard for business insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and analysis
* **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development
* **Data Analysis** – KPI and business performance analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

* Total Revenue KPI
* Total Profit KPI
* Total Units Sold KPI
* Average Shipping Days KPI
* Understock KPI
* Inventory Status Analysis
* Top 10 Products by Units Sold
* Supplier Performance Analysis
* Revenue by Category
* Stock Available vs Units Sold
* Product-wise Revenue and Profit Analysis

## 💡 Key Insights

* Total Revenue: **3.08B**
* Total Profit: **4.14M**
* Total Units Sold: **2.22M**
* Average Shipping Time: **5.5 days**
* Overstock was the dominant inventory status, with **11,985 records**
* **2,992 records** were identified as understock
* **Camera** was the top-selling product and generated the highest product profit
* **Fashion** generated the highest revenue among the categories
* **Supplier E** recorded the highest average shipping time

## 🔎 Business Questions Addressed

1. Which products are frequently out of stock?
2. Which suppliers have the highest shipping times?
3. Which products generate the highest profit?
4. How can inventory be optimized?
5. What is the impact of shipping delays on sales?
